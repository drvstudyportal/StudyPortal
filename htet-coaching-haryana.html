Guiding Educators. Building Futures

Study Portal Academy prepares candidates for the Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) conducted by the Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH), Bhiwani — Level 1 (PRT), Level 2 (TGT) and Level 3 (PGT). Preparation covers child development and pedagogy, the language papers, subject content, and the Haryana general-knowledge and reasoning component that distinguishes HTET from CTET. Classes are bilingual in Hindi and English and reach every Haryana district live online, with recordings for replay.

Frequently asked questions

Study Portal Academy runs HTET preparation for Level 1 (PRT), Level 2 (TGT) and Level 3 (PGT), delivered bilingually in Hindi and English and available online across every Haryana district — Rohtak, Hisar, Panipat, Sonipat, Karnal, Ambala, Bhiwani, Jind, Sirsa, Kurukshetra, Rewari, Faridabad and Gurugram included. It pairs HTET coaching with follow-on tracks for HSSC/HPSC teaching recruitment and for KVS, NVS and EMRS posts.

CTET is the central eligibility test conducted by CBSE and qualifies you for central schools such as KVS and NVS. HTET is conducted by the Board of School Education Haryana and is required for Haryana state school teaching posts; it includes a Haryana-specific general-knowledge and reasoning section that CTET does not have. Many aspirants prepare for both.

Level 1 is for primary teacher (PRT) posts, Level 2 for trained graduate teacher (TGT) posts and Level 3 for post graduate teacher (PGT) posts. Eligibility depends on your qualification — confirm against the current BSEH notification.
