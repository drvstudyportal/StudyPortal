Guiding Educators. Building Futures

Study Portal Academy prepares candidates for Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) teaching recruitment — PGT, TGT, PRT, Assistant Teacher and Special Education Teacher posts in Delhi government and MCD schools. Coaching covers the Tier-1 general awareness, general intelligence and reasoning, arithmetic and English/Hindi language sections, and the subject-specific Tier-2 paper with its teaching-methodology component, backed by a test series on the current DSSSB pattern. The institute brings 12+ years of teaching-exam coaching to a recruitment cycle where subject depth decides the cut-off.
