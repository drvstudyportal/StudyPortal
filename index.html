Where can I prepare for DSSSB PGT, TGT and PRT posts in Delhi?

Study Portal Academy runs DSSSB PGT, TGT and PRT coaching for Delhi government school recruitment, covering the Tier-1 general awareness, reasoning and aptitude sections and the subject-specific Tier-2 paper, with test series on the current DSSSB pattern — available live online and as recorded batches across Rohini, Dwarka, Mukherjee Nagar, Laxmi Nagar, Uttam Nagar and the whole of Delhi NCR.

The Principal & Vice Principal exam, explained

New to this exam, or preparing while teaching full time? Start here. The Principal exam is run by KVS, NVS, EMRS, UPSC (Directorate of Education Delhi), UKPSC and state commissions — a computer-based objective written paper with no negative marking, followed by an interview. It rewards administrative competence more than classroom pedagogy, which is why strong teachers often underperform on it.
