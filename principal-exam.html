Who conducts the Principal exam

“The Principal exam” is not one examination. It is a family of recruitment exams run by different bodies, sharing most of a syllabus. A candidate preparing seriously usually applies to several in the same year.

The selection process

1. Written examination

Computer-based and objective, set across two papers. There is no negative marking, so an unattempted question costs more than a guess.

2. Interview

Candidates who clear the written stage are called to interview, which explores your vision for running a school — commonly through a school development plan and a question-and-answer round.

3. Document verification

Qualifications, experience and category claims are verified before appointment.

Question counts, marks, durations and the exact written-to-interview weightage are fixed in each recruitment notification and differ between bodies and between cycles. Always confirm them against the official advertisement for the cycle you are applying to.

What the syllabus actually covers

Perspectives on Education & Leadership

Educational philosophy, policy including NEP and RTE, curriculum planning, pedagogy, and school leadership theory. This is the heart of the paper and the section that most often separates selected candidates from the rest.

Administration & Finance

School administration, financial rules and procedures, budgeting, audit, purchase and store rules. Study Portal Academy runs a post-specific Administration & Finance batch and a dedicated mock test because this section is where well-prepared teachers most often lose marks.

Central Government Service Rules

Conduct rules, leave rules, pension and service conditions applicable to central government employees — directly relevant to running a Kendriya Vidyalaya or Navodaya Vidyalaya.

General Awareness & Current Affairs

National and international current affairs, education-sector developments, government schemes and general knowledge.

Reasoning & Quantitative Aptitude

Logical and analytical reasoning, data interpretation and numerical ability at the level expected of a senior school administrator.

Language Proficiency — English & Hindi

Comprehension, grammar and usage in both languages, matching the bilingual medium in which these schools operate.

Frequently asked questions

The Principal exam is a recruitment examination for the post of Principal — and, in the same cycle, Vice Principal — in government and government-aided school systems. In India it is conducted by Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS), Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), Eklavya Model Residential Schools through NESTS, the Union Public Service Commission for the Directorate of Education Delhi, UKPSC, and various state public service commissions. Selection runs through a written examination followed by an interview and document verification.

The written stage is computer-based and objective, set across two papers, with no negative marking for wrong answers. The papers test language proficiency in English and Hindi, perspectives on education and leadership, general awareness, reasoning and quantitative aptitude, and administration and finance. Candidates who clear the written stage are called for an interview, and final selection combines the two. Exact question counts, marks, durations and weightages are set in each recruitment notification and should be confirmed against the official advertisement for the cycle you are applying to.

Broadly six areas: perspectives on education and leadership; administration and finance; central government service rules; general awareness and current affairs; reasoning and quantitative aptitude; and English and Hindi language proficiency. Administration, finance and service rules carry real weight and are the sections most serving teachers underestimate, because they sit outside day-to-day classroom work.

Eligibility typically requires a master's degree, a B.Ed or equivalent teaching qualification, and a minimum number of years of relevant experience — often as a PGT, Vice Principal or in an equivalent administrative role — along with an upper age limit. The exact qualification, experience and age requirements differ between KVS, NVS, EMRS, UPSC and state recruitment, and change between cycles, so always verify against the official notification before applying.
